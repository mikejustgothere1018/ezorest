Welcome to Ezo Kitchen & Sushi Bar, where you can get umami sushi from Japan, savory pho from Vietnam, and sweet and salty pad thai from Thailand ALL IN ONE STOP!

We serve a variety of dishes inspired by different Asian cultures. Whether you're looking for sushi, noodles or a delicious curry, we have something for you.

About Us

Our Story

Ezo Kitchen & Sushi Bar combines Japanese, Thai and Vietnamese cuisines in one restaurant.

Our goal is to bring people together through food and introduce customers to different Asian cultures.

Meet Our Owners

Our two owners come from Japanese-American and Afro-Latino backgrounds. Together, they created Ezo to share their love of food and culture.

They wanted to create a restaurant where people could enjoy foods from several Asian countries in one place. Ezo is about good food, cultural appreciation and bringing people together.
